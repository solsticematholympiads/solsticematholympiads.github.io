About Us

Mission Statement

Solstice Math Olympiads gives students 18 and under hard, carefully written math problems at no cost.

Every problem is written and test-solved by students on our team, and we run each contest online so anyone can take part.

History

  1. 2021

    • Founded by a group of middle school students who loved competition math
    • Held the first WSMO online on December 21, 2021, with about 50 participants

  2. 2022

    • Held the first SSMO on June 27, 2022, introducing the Relay Round
    • Drew nearly 100 participants to the first SSMO

  3. 2023

    • Brought WSMO back on December 29, 2023, after a year off
    • Ran SSMO over a weekend on gclocked, a timed Google Form platform

  4. 2024

    • Formed a new problem-writing team
    • Rebuilt the website
    • Grew SSMO to seven sponsors, up from five in 2023

  5. 2025

    • Held SSMO on June 21 and WSMO on December 27, both on Zoom
