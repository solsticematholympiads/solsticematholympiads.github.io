Our Team

SMO is run by three student contest directors.

Anthony Zou

Contest Director
Lead Problem Writer

Anthony Zou is a contest director and SMO's lead problem writer. A two-time USAJMO qualifier (Honorable Mention) and two-time USAMO qualifier, Anthony also plays the flute and piano.

Aivin Le

Contest Director

Aivin Le studies biochemistry at Siena University. A two-time AIME qualifier, Aivin is a former MATHCOUNTS coach and member of the Albany-area math circle, and runs the contest's logistics behind the scenes.

Aakaash Nattanmai

Contest Director

Aakaash Nattanmai studies computer science and math at Cornell University. Aakaash has qualified for USACO Platinum, the AIME, and MathCON Nationals, and was a nationally qualified Lincoln-Douglas debater.

Each contest's problem writers and test solvers are listed on its team page.
