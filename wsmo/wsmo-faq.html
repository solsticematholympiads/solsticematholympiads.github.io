WSMO FAQ

When will WSMO be held?
The 2025 WSMO took place on Saturday, December 27, 2025. The date of the next WSMO will be announced on our Discord.
What rounds does WSMO have?
WSMO has four rounds:
  • Speed Round: 10 questions in 30 minutes
  • Accuracy Round: 10 questions in 60 minutes
  • Team Round: 15 questions in 60 minutes
  • Tiebreaker Round: 3 questions in up to 30 minutes (used only to break ties)
The Speed, Accuracy, and Tiebreaker rounds are taken individually. The Team Round is taken together, and each team manages its own communication during the contest.
What kind of questions does WSMO have?
All questions are short answer, and every answer is an integer.
How hard is WSMO?
Problems range from early AMC 8 to late AIME difficulty. To see for yourself, try our problems on the Past Contests page.
