Summer Solstice Math Olympiad

Our free summer contest, held online each June.

Overview

SSMO has five rounds: Speed, Accuracy, Team, Relay, and a Tiebreaker used only to break ties. Every answer is an integer, and the contest is free and open to students 18 and under.

For dates, rounds, and difficulty, see the FAQs.

SMO FAQ SSMO FAQ

History

SSMO was first held on June 27, 2022, as a summer counterpart to WSMO.

It ran every June from 2022 through 2025. Every problem is written by students. Hundreds of participants from the U.S. and abroad have taken part, with prizes from as many as seven sponsors.
